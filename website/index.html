      GPIO port test

      For testing only. These buttons switch raw relay ports and bypass the scheduler. Pulse 1s on a power port waits its turn in the drive queue for the capacitor to charge. On/Off are refused while a valve drive is in progress. Don't leave a power port on.

      To test a full valve move by hand: turn the direction port on (open) or off (close), then pulse the power port.
