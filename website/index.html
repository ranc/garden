GPIO port test

For testing only. These buttons switch raw relay ports and bypass the scheduler. Don't leave a power port on: the solenoid coil is meant for short pulses. Use Pulse 1s for power ports. The scheduler turns ports off whenever it drives a valve.

To test a full valve move by hand: turn the direction port on (open) or off (close), then pulse the power port.
